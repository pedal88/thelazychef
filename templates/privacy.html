Privacy

What we keep about you

Last changed 31 October 2026.

The Lazy Chef is a recipe site made and run by Pål Andreas Dahl in Norway, who is responsible for your data. Write to thelazychefai@gmail.com about anything on this page.

Without an account

You can read and cook every recipe without telling us anything. Your ticked-off steps stay in your own browser. When you come from one of our TikTok or Instagram posts, we count one visit for that post, per day, with nothing that says who you are.

With an account

  • Your email address, and your password, stored only as a one-way hash we cannot read back.
  • If you sign in with Google: the ID of your Google account and the email Google gives us. We ask Google for nothing else.
  • When you signed up, your answer to "How did you find us?", and which post you came from, if any.
  • What you do with recipes: the ones you save, put in Cook next or mark as made, and your stars, notes and photos.
  • If you pair with someone in Mirror, they see your email and the recipes you have both saved.

We use this only to give you your account and to see, in numbers, which posts bring people who cook. We do not sell it, show ads or use it to train AI models.

Photos

A photo you add to a recipe is stored at an unlisted web address so that your page can show it. It is not linked from anywhere, but anyone who has the address can open it. Removing a photo from your note takes it off your page; to have the file itself deleted, write to us.

Cookies

Three, all for the site itself: one for your visit, one that remembers metric or US units if you choose, and one that keeps you signed in on this device for up to a year, until you log out. No tracking or advertising cookies.

Where it is kept, and who else sees it

  • Everything is stored with Google Cloud in the EU (Stockholm and Belgium). Google keeps the site's request logs (address, browser and time of each request) for 30 days.
  • Pages load fonts from Google Fonts and styles from Tailwind's servers, which see your IP address as with any web request.
  • The recipes and their pictures are written and drawn by AI (Anthropic's Claude and Google's Gemini). Nothing you write or upload is sent to them.

Your rights

Write to us to see what we have about you, to correct it, or to have your account and everything in it deleted; we answer within 30 days. You can also complain to the Norwegian Data Protection Authority, Datatilsynet. We keep your data for as long as you have an account.
